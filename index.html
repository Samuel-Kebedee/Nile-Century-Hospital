Establishment Story of
Nile Century Hospital

The idea of establishing Nile Century Hospital was initiated by Dr. Fikru Bekele, with the vision of creating a quality, patient-centered healthcare institution. The institution was subsequently legally established through the collective efforts of Dr. Fikru Bekele, Dr. Robel Wondisho, Dr. Yerosan Tadesse, and Dr. Ayana Guto.

Following its legal establishment, other specialist doctors and supportive professionals joined the initiative, contributing their expertise, resources, and commitment to the growth of the institution. Dr. Fikru Bekele was elected to serve as the first CEO, while Dr. Yosan Tadesse was elected to serve as the first Medical Director.

Today, Nile Century Hospital has grown into a professional partnership of 32 members, united by a shared vision and commitment to providing quality healthcare services.

Patient Guide for New Patients

What to bring

ID card, Credit Organization Card/Letter, insurance card, previous medical papers and a list of your medicines.

How your first visit works

Register at reception, see the nurse for basic checks on Triage, pay at casier, then meet your doctor.

Payment

Pay at the cashier before tests or medicines. Credit aorganization and Insurance is accepted for partners.

Arrive early

Come 15 minutes before your appointment for registration.
